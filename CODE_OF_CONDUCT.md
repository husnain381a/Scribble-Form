# Community code of conduct

Scribble welcomes people of different backgrounds, experiences, and skill levels. Help make the project a place where someone can ask a first question, disagree about a design, and contribute without being belittled.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient. Give feedback about the work, with useful examples.
- Assume good faith while taking reports of harm seriously.
- Respect boundaries, privacy, and other people's time.
- Credit others' work and keep discussions relevant to the project.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, unwanted sexual attention, targeted insults, and publishing someone's private information are not acceptable. Neither are repeated disruption, spam, or retaliation against someone who raises a concern.

## Reporting and moderation

For an ordinary discussion problem, use GitHub's reporting tools or ask a maintainer for help. For a sensitive concern, contact a maintainer through a private channel listed on their GitHub profile. If no channel is listed, ask how to contact a maintainer privately without posting the incident details or anyone's personal information.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close or lock discussions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Responses should consider the severity, context, and repeated nature of the behavior. Reports are handled as privately as practical; confidentiality or a response deadline cannot be guaranteed.

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces officially maintained for Scribble.
